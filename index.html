Measuring user experience with numbers

In this activity you will play a game, rate it with a short standardised questionnaire (the UEQ-S), and then see how the whole class's answers are combined, visualised and interpreted. It is a small, complete example of quantitative data collection and analysis.

Start the survey View class results

The process, step by step

  1. Experience

    Play the game for the agreed time. This is the "treatment": everyone experiences the same product before measuring.

  2. Measure

    Answer the 8 UEQ-S items. Each is a pair of opposite words on a 7-point scale. A standardised instrument means results can be compared across studies.

  3. Collect

    Every response is stored against an anonymous participant number, so data can be checked and corrected without names.

  4. Score

    Answers 1 to 7 are re-coded to −3 to +3. Items are averaged into two dimensions, pragmatic and hedonic quality, plus an overall score.

  5. Analyse

    Box plots show the spread of scores. Means, confidence intervals and a reliability check describe the data.

  6. Interpret

    Scores are judged against the UEQ's neutral band (−0.8 to +0.8) and a benchmark of 246 products rated by 9,905 people.

What the UEQ-S measures

Pragmatic quality

Goal-directed, task-related quality. Items: obstructive–supportive, complicated–easy, inefficient–efficient, confusing–clear.

Hedonic quality

Non-task, pleasure-related quality. Items: boring–exciting, not interesting–interesting, conventional–inventive, usual–leading edge.

Overall

The mean of all eight items, a single summary of the user experience.

About this tool

This site uses the UEQ-S, the short version of the User Experience Questionnaire, developed by Martin Schrepp, Andreas Hinderks and Jörg Thomaschewski. The questionnaire, the benchmark and further material are freely available at ueq-online.org.

  • Schrepp, M., Hinderks, A. & Thomaschewski, J. (2017). Design and evaluation of a short version of the User Experience Questionnaire (UEQ-S). International Journal of Interactive Multimedia and Artificial Intelligence, 4(6), 103–108.
  • Hinderks, A., Schrepp, M. & Thomaschewski, J. A benchmark for the short version of the User Experience Questionnaire.
  • Laugwitz, B., Held, T. & Schrepp, M. (2008). Construction and evaluation of a user experience questionnaire. In HCI and Usability for Education and Work (USAB 2008), LNCS 5298, 63–76. Springer.
